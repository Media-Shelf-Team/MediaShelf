What is MEDIA SHELF?

Media Shelf is your personal space to track, organize, and discover the entertainment you love. Keep all your favorite movies, TV shows, books, audiobooks, video games, and music together in one convenient place.

Build your collection, keep track of what you've enjoyed, and explore new favorites. As Media Shelf grows, you'll also be able to connect with friends and discover what they're watching, reading, playing, and listening to.
