About Media Shelf

Media Shelf is a place to keep track of the entertainment you love, all in one space. It tracks movies, TV shows, video games, books, audiobooks, and music. Instead of juggling separate lists, users can create a profile and organize their media in one place. Our goal is to make personal media tracking simple, useful, and enjoyable.
